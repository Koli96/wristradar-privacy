WristRadar privacy policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

WristRadar is a Wear OS app with a companion phone app. A button on the watch makes the paired Android phone ring loudly so that you can find it.

No personal data is collected

WristRadar collects no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

No servers, nothing sent to the developer

WristRadar has no servers and sends nothing to the developer or to any third party. The phone app and the watch app talk only to each other, through the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services, normally directly over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi between your paired phone and watch. The app does not need an internet connection. The messages only ask the phone to ring or stop ringing and report whether it is ringing.

Settings stay on your device

The app keeps its settings and state only on your phone and watch. Nothing is stored on servers or shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

The app asks only for what it needs to ring the phone: for example notifications, Do Not Disturb access and linking your watch as a companion device. These are used on the device to make the phone audible, and no data from them leaves your devices.

Purchases

If the app offers purchases, they are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the wristradar-privacy repository on GitHub.
